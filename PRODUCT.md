# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary:** immigrant women — Brazilian and Latin American women living abroad (U.S. and elsewhere) who see their own lives in stories of other women who rebuilt themselves across borders. They come to read the magazine, recognize themselves, and possibly share their own story.

**Equally important business audience:** brands, businesses, and organizations that want to advertise in the magazine or partner with it. The single most important action on the site is an advertiser/partner getting in touch.

## Product Purpose

The official portal of the magazine **A Mulher de Aço / The Woman of Steel**. The magazine comes first, then reporting and projects. Central message (from the founder): the magazine "is born from one woman's story, but exists to give visibility to the stories of many women."

Success means: advertisers and partners reach out; readers open/buy the edition; women discover and submit stories.

## Positioning

A quarterly, bilingual, international magazine made by and about immigrant women, with an editorial voice rooted in lived experience of migration ("Beyond Borders, Beyond Limits"). Not a personal biography site of the founder — the founder appears as editorial signature and origin, not as the product.

## Operating Context

- Quarterly editions published as flipbooks on Heyzine (current: https://heyzine.com/flip-book/3a26311c88.html).
- Founder Maria Barile (writer/journalist, Brazilian, based in Connecticut) must approve anything published about her. Content needs a draft → approval flow.
- This Next.js repository is a **prototype for client approval**; the final site will be **rebuilt in Wix**. Layouts must be simple sections that are easy to reproduce in Wix — no complex custom functionality.
- Domain: **amulherdeaco.com**.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Navigation: Home, The Magazine (quarterly editions via Heyzine), Reporting (by editorial section), Women of Steel, About the Founder, Events, Partnerships & Advertising, Contact.
- **Women of Steel**: both editorial profiles written by the magazine team and a form where readers can nominate/submit stories, with approval before publication.
- **Shop**: e-book, magazine editions, and other products the founder can add herself from an admin panel (in Wix).
- **Partnerships & Advertising**: primary conversion — contact/inquiry path for advertisers and partners.
- Languages: **English is primary**; Portuguese at launch; structure ready for Spanish, Italian, French. Other languages via automatic translation (Google Translate style).
- Undecided (pending Maria): advertising rates/media kit, events calendar, partners list, submission-form fields.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "A Mulher de Aço / The Woman of Steel". Slogan: "Beyond Borders, Beyond Limits".
- Founder's binding decision: **institutional** tone over purely editorial/handmade; the magazine leads, the founder is the editorial signature (founder's letter, bylines, own page).
- The printed magazine's identity exists (dark navy and gold, gold serif title with a globe in the "O") and must be respected; visual decisions are made later in DESIGN.md.

## Product Principles

1. The magazine is the center; every page leads back to the edition.
2. Many women's stories, not one woman's biography.
3. Make advertising and partnership the easiest path on the site for businesses.
4. Nothing about Maria or any featured woman goes live without approval.
5. Build what Wix can reproduce; simplicity over custom cleverness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Multilingual readers, many reading in a second language: plain language, clear hierarchy, readable type sizes, and language switching that is easy to find.
